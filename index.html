About Me

I am a student from Riverside Secondary School driven by a passion for leadership, service, and the humanities. Recognised among others for my love for Literature, I bring analytical depth and articulate communication to everything I pursue. I seek to look beyond concepts and put them into my own words, forming an understanding of my own in what I do.

Beyond the classroom, I serve as a student leader championing school initiatives, express my creativity through the Drama CCA, and dedicate my time as a community volunteer under Resident Networks and the Northwest CDC. Being trilingual in English, Mandarin, and Tamil allows me to connect authentically with people across diverse backgrounds, cultivating a deeper knowledge of varying practices here in Singapore.
